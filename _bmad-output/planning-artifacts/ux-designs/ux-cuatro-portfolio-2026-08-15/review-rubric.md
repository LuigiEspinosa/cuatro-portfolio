# Spine Pair Review — cuatro-portfolio

## Overall verdict

This is a substantive, unusually well-reasoned pair: every UJ is mirrored verbatim with a real climax beat, the palette is authored in OKLCH with contrast ratios that are computed rather than claimed (two spot-checks reproduce to two decimals), and the three invented sections — Seams Inventory, Asset Budget, Open Items — each answer an explicit deliverable in the UX prompt. The shape is sound and the decisions are committed.

What blocks clean source-extraction is the join between the two files. DESIGN.md carries two disjoint token surfaces — a YAML frontmatter subset and a much larger `tokens.css` code block — and EXPERIENCE.md's twenty `{path.to.token}` references resolve against neither, while three load-bearing values (nav underline weight, focus-token reuse, `Archived` at 70% opacity) contradict across sections. Add one live route (`/projects`) with no disposition and mockups linked only as an undifferentiated header pair, and a fresh `bmad-architecture` run will need a hand-reconciliation pass before it can trust the contract.

## 1. Flow coverage — strong

Extracted UJ-1…UJ-4 from PRD §2.3 and checked each against § Key Flows. All four are present, in source order, with protagonist names verbatim (Daniela, Marcus, Cuatro, Ana), the PRD's own journey titles carried across word-for-word, numbered steps, an explicit `**Climax — step N**` line, a `**Resolution**` line and an `**Edge case**` that matches the PRD's edge case in substance. v2 flows (UJ-3, UJ-4) are marked as deferred at the top of the flow rather than silently included. No UJ misses.

### Findings

- **medium** Flow headings carry no `UJ-n` identifier — traceability runs only through the protagonist name, and the note "Protagonist names are taken verbatim from PRD §2.3" is the sole binding (§ Key Flows, all four headings). A story-dev consumer cutting acceptance criteria per journey has to infer the mapping. *Fix:* prefix each heading, e.g. `### UJ-1 — Daniela decides in four minutes…`.
- **medium** Every flow closes with a `**Measured by**` line citing SM-1/SM-2/SM-3, but FR-34 — which requires that "reaching the Suite Directory," "opening a `live` link" and "opening a `source` link" be *distinguishable events* — is cited nowhere in either spine, and no interaction in § Component Patterns or § Interaction Primitives is marked as an instrumentation boundary. FR-34 is MVP scope (PRD §9.1). *Fix:* add a short instrumentation table naming the three events and the exact interactions that emit them; leave the mechanism to architecture.
- **medium** FR-27 (`demo` declaration per Registry Entry, "states what a Visitor can do with that application before they click") has no surface. § Component Patterns → Registry Entry fixes reading order as name → status → description → tech → links with no demo slot, and the switcher data contract omits the field. PRD §9.2 defers FR-25–27 but explicitly keeps the part applications already satisfy. *Fix:* either give `demo` a rendering position in the entry or state in one line that it is unrendered at MVP and why.

## 2. Token completeness — thin

Extracted the DESIGN.md YAML frontmatter (10 colours, 8 typography roles, 4 radii, 11 spacing entries, 4 component entries), the `tokens.css` block (≈70 custom properties across colour, families, type scale, weight, line-height, tracking, measure, space, shape, stroke, elevation, motion, layer), and all 20 `{…}` references in EXPERIENCE.md. Every colour token carries a hex value and a role. Contrast is stated for all load-bearing pairs and a 15-pair conformance matrix is published; `muted` on `paper` recomputes to 7.02:1 against the claimed 7.03, and `line-strong` on `paper` to 3.50:1 against the claimed 3.52 — the numbers are real, not decorative.

### Findings

- **critical** 19 of the 20 `{…}` references in EXPERIENCE.md do not resolve to any path in DESIGN.md's frontmatter: `{color.accent}` ×4 and `{color.focus}` ×2 (frontmatter key is `colors`, plural); `{measure}` (lives at `spacing.measure`); `{stroke.focus}` ×2, `{focus.offset}` ×2, `{z.sticky}`, `{dur.micro|minor|major|exit}`, `{ease.entrance|exit|toggle}` (no such frontmatter keys exist at all); and `{tokens.css}` ×2, which is a *filename* dressed in token syntax (§ Foundation table, § Component Patterns → Suite Switcher). DESIGN.md itself uses zero `{path.to.token}` references — its prose addresses tokens as `var(--color-border)`. The `{token}` binding is the entire contract between the two files and it currently resolves for nothing. *Fix:* pick one addressing scheme. Cheapest is to make the frontmatter the resolution target and rewrite EXPERIENCE.md refs to real paths (`{colors.accent}`, `{spacing.measure}`, …), after closing the gap below; replace `{tokens.css}` with plain backticks.
- **high** The frontmatter is a strict subset of the system. Absent from it but present in `tokens.css` and depended on by EXPERIENCE.md: motion (`--dur-*`, `--ease-*`), z-index (`--z-*`), stroke widths (`--stroke-*`, `--focus-offset`), elevation aliases (`--elev-*`), font families (`--f-*`), weights (`--w-*`), line-heights (`--lh-*`), tracking (`--tr-*`), and the whole `--t-*` scale. A consumer honouring the design.md spec's promise that frontmatter is the machine-readable layer extracts an incomplete system and silently loses reduced-motion, focus and layering values. *Fix:* lift the missing families into frontmatter (nested objects are fine per spec) and keep `tokens.css` as the generated projection, not a second source.
- **high** `Archived` status renders as "the same at 70% opacity" (§ Components → Status pill), which composites `line-strong` over `paper` to ≈2.75:1 — below the WCAG 1.4.11 3:1 floor the spine names as the reason `line-strong` was raised in the first place, and below what § Colors → Conformance asserts holds for every meaningful pairing. Its label text lands at ≈5.2:1, under the stated 7:1 body target. The Status border is explicitly meaning-bearing (FR-7, A-3). Not rendered in the Suite Directory at MVP (FR-35 filters it) but rendered in `mockups/key-screens.html` S5 and reachable by any consumer that renders all four values. *Fix:* give `Archived` its own opaque token pair instead of an opacity multiplier, and add the pair to the conformance matrix.
- **medium** `components:` in frontmatter maps each name to a prose string (`status-pill: outlined, never filled`). The spec requires an object of component tokens mapped to values or `{path.to.token}` references; a resolver that flattens component tokens at consumption time gets a sentence. *Fix:* convert to objects, or drop the key and leave § Components as the sole component surface.
- **low** No `description` key in DESIGN.md frontmatter (spec lists it as the one-line statement of what the system is). *Fix:* one line.
- **low** `--t-lg` (25px) and `--t-2xl` (39px) are published with "—" in the "Used for" column (§ Typography → Scale). Two scale steps that no role consumes are two more names in a contract where a rename is a major break. *Fix:* assign or delete.

## 3. Component coverage — thin

Extracted every component named in either file. DESIGN.md § Components: Status pill · Registry Entry · Links · Button · Framework band · Plate mark · Nav · Suite Switcher panel. EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns: Registry Entry · Status mark · Tracker Family group · Suite Switcher · Nav · Skip control. Only Registry Entry, Nav and the Switcher appear on both sides. Where a component does appear on both sides, the rules are real — the Registry Entry pair in particular (reading order vs tab order, two independent 44px targets, no whole-row target) is the strongest single piece of the pair.

### Findings

- **high** The same component is called **Status pill** in DESIGN.md (§ Components, § Colors, frontmatter `status-pill`) and **Status mark** in EXPERIENCE.md (§ Component Patterns, § Voice and Tone context). *Fix:* pick one — `Status mark` reads better against the "outlined, never filled" rule — and sweep both files plus the frontmatter key.
- **high** **Tracker Family group** has a full behavioural spec (labelled container, framing line names no count, not collapsible, not a tab set) and *no* visual spec anywhere in DESIGN.md. It is the only containment layer in the entire directory (FR-11) and therefore the one place a border, an inset and a label type ramp have to be decided. `mockups/key-screens.html` S3 shows one, but the spine wins on conflict and the spine is silent. *Fix:* add a Tracker Family group row to § Components.
- **medium** **Skip control** likewise: behaviour is specified (above the fold, moves focus not scroll, distinct from the a11y skip-link) with no visual row, though it is load-bearing for FR-2's one-interaction requirement and appears styled in the mock. *Fix:* add a row, or state that it inherits the Button spec.
- **medium** **Links**, **Button**, **Framework band** and **Plate mark** are visual-only — no behavioural row in § Component Patterns. Links matter most: the accent-underline vs hairline-underline hierarchy is what makes one entry serve both readers, and its hover/focus/visited behaviour is currently only inferable from § State Patterns → Registry Entry. Button has no named consumer surface at all in the Hub. *Fix:* add behavioural rows for Links; for Button, Framework band and Plate mark, one line each stating "no behaviour — static" is enough and closes the check.
- **low** DESIGN.md says **Suite Switcher panel**, EXPERIENCE.md says **Suite Switcher**. Defensible (panel is a part of the pattern) but reads as drift next to the Status pill/mark case. *Fix:* say "Suite Switcher — panel".

## 4. State coverage — adequate

Walked every surface in the § Information Architecture tree: `/`, `#suite`, embedded Connect Four, `/cv`, `/work`, `/recommendation`, `/celeste`, `/api/health`, plus the Suite Switcher inside a Satellite. Suite Directory (loaded / registry unreachable / empty-is-a-defect / dead `live` URL), Registry Entry (rest / hover / focus-visible / active / visited) and the narrative (loading / failed / blocked / reduced-motion) are covered thoroughly, and the § Global block's three explicit *absences* — no toast system, no confirmation dialogs, no skeletons — are exactly the kind of committed negative a story-dev consumer needs.

### Findings

- **high** `/projects` is missing entirely from both spines. It is a live route on the shipping v2.5.3 site (addendum §C.2 route list; `app/projects/page.tsx` renders the existing registry) and the UX prompt names it among today's routes. The reshape moves the registry to the homepage, so `/projects` either becomes a redirect, a duplicate or a deletion — and NFR-2 says nothing live may break. *Fix:* add a row to the § IA navigation-prominence table stating its disposition.
- **medium** `/cv`, `/work`, `/recommendation` and `/celeste` appear in the IA tree and the prominence table and then get nothing — no states, no component patterns, no layout. Q8's closure says `/work` "folds into `/cv` as a section" *while surviving as its own route*, which produces two URLs serving the same content with no canonical, no redirect and no statement of which is authoritative. *Fix:* one line per route on the canonical/duplicate question; the rest can legitimately be declared spine-only.
- **medium** No error-route state anywhere (404, or a `live` subdomain typo landing on the Hub). The Hub is a public website with real Visitors and a nav that must not disappear. *Fix:* one row in § State Patterns → Global.
- **low** Suite Switcher states live only in § Component Patterns (unreachable Registry → trigger does not render; Escape returns focus). Open/closed, in-flight fetch and single-entry cases are not walked in § State Patterns. *Fix:* mirror the switcher into the State Patterns table.
- **low** Embedded Connect Four appears in the IA tree (`[Connect Four] v2, embedded (FR-29)`) with no behaviour, no states and no component. Defensible as v2, but it is the only IA node with zero downstream text. *Fix:* mark it `v2 — not specified in this run`, as the switcher and identity flows are.

## 5. Visual reference coverage — thin

### Findings

- **high** No inline links and no naming of what each render illustrates. `key-screens.html` contains six labelled screens — S1 narrative handoff, S2 the non-3D front door, S3 Suite Directory mobile, S4 Suite Directory desktop, S5 Registry Entry anatomy with all four Status values plus hover and focus-visible, S6 Suite Switcher in a Satellite — and nothing in either spine points § IA → S1/S2, § The Suite Directory → S3/S4, § Component Patterns → Registry Entry → S5, or § Suite Switcher → S6. The single most useful visual reference in the workspace is effectively unaddressable. *Fix:* replace the header block with per-section inline links naming the screen, e.g. `→ Composition reference: mockups/key-screens.html#S5 — Registry Entry anatomy, all four Status values.`
- **high** `directions-4.html` is a Discovery decision artifact, not a reference render: it shows four directions of which three (A Terminal Ledger, B Editorial Specimen, D Quiet Instrument) were rejected, on foreign anchor hues (295, 312), and its Direction C swatches are pre-correction (`paper 12%` ✔ but `surface 15.5%` against the spine's 16%, and none of the raised `line-strong`/`muted` values). Publishing it in `mockups/` and linking it from a spine as a peer of `key-screens.html` invites a downstream reader to extract a superseded palette or the wrong direction. *Fix:* leave it in `.working/`, or link it with an explicit label — "superseded exploration; Direction C values here predate the contrast correction."
- **low** `.working/` retains byte-identical copies of both promoted files. Harmless, but a second copy of a token block is a second thing to drift. *Fix:* optional.
- **low** `key-screens.html` L35 comments `--t-xs: 0.8125rem; /* 14px floor for body copy */` — it is 13px, and the spine's body floor is `--t-sm` at 14px. Mock-internal only. *Fix:* correct the comment if the file is touched again.

## 6. Bloat & overspecification — adequate

Checked both files for source restatement, pixel specs where a token exists, prose where a table works, and sections with no downstream consumer. Most of the length is earned: the UX prompt asked for the token contract with real values, the SCSS-to-token migration path, the seams inventory and an asset budget as named deliverables, and all four are here in table form. Tables are used well throughout — the seams inventory, the migration mapping, the per-framework consumption table and the accessibility floor would all be worse as prose.

### Findings

- **medium** § Foundation restates the vendor precedent in full — Material Web defunded June 2024, `primer/view_components` retired February 2026 citing React, Adobe maintaining two implementations. That paragraph is PRD §4.4 and research §D2 verbatim in substance, and both are named sources. *Fix:* cut to the conclusion plus a source pointer; the ceiling statement ("reads as one author, not feels like one product") is the part that does work here.
- **medium** § The Token Contract → **Build** (Style Dictionary 5.5.1, the `convertTokenData` prototype-pollution patch, DTCG 2025.10, Terrazzo rejected at 0.7.2) and → **Versioning** (NL Design System convention, deprecate → migrate → remove) are research §D2 restated, and are build-tooling and release-policy decisions that a fresh `bmad-architecture` run owns. A UX spine that pre-empts them creates two places to keep in sync. *Fix:* compress to "per research §D2" plus the one delta this design adds (the third file), which is genuinely new and belongs here.
- **medium** § Voice and Tone publishes `Six applications · five languages · one operator` as a footer string under a rule that says "every number in the interface is a real count" and "never invent a metric." Across the six *rendered* entries the language count is not five — five languages is an estate-wide figure (PRD §1) covering apps the Suite Directory deliberately does not render (FR-35). `mockups/key-screens.html` S4 repeats it as `6 running · 5 languages`. This is precisely the claim Marcus checks. *Fix:* drop the language count or scope it explicitly ("five languages across the estate").
- **low** EXPERIENCE.md carries editorial voice in a few places where DESIGN.md should own it — "a still of a 3D scene reads as a *broken* 3D scene", "a documented seam reads as judgement while a discovered one reads as an oversight", "Purple is not the problem. Purple *used as a fill* is." Each is attached to a real decision, so none is decorative, but the register belongs in DESIGN.md § Brand & Style. *Fix:* low priority; trim on the next pass.

### Findings

The spines contradict each other on a token whose whole value is that its appearance means one thing. *Fix:* use `--color-accent` for the hover brighten, or relax the DESIGN.md rule deliberately.
- **high** The current-nav underline is specified three times at two weights: `1px` accent underline (DESIGN.md § Components → Nav), `2px solid var(--color-accent)` for "current-nav mark" (DESIGN.md § Elevation & Depth → border treatments, the Emphasis row), and `2px {color.accent}` (EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns → Nav). DESIGN.md contradicts itself, so spines-win-on-conflict resolves nothing. *Fix:* settle on `--stroke-emphasis` (2px) and reference the token rather than restating a pixel value in three places.
- **medium** Dark-only overrides a stated source constraint — the UX prompt fixes "**Light and dark must both be defined.** Several satellites already have themes" — and neither spine records it *as* an override of a named source. DESIGN.md § Colors states the decision and points to seam S-7; S-7 describes the consequence for `digital-library` and `cs-tracker` but never says which source requirement was overridden or that the user held the position after being shown the collision (which `.memlog.md` records). - **medium** § Component Patterns → Suite Switcher → Data contract states "A Satellite needs exactly four fields to render a switcher row" directly under a table marking five fields required (`id`, `name`, `status`, `tech`, `source`) plus `live` when `Live`. *Fix:* make the sentence agree with the table, or mark `source` as contract-only in the required column.
- **low** Three glossary terms never appear in either spine: **Capacity Gate**, **Demo Account** and — nearly — **Visitor**, which is used twice while the spines otherwise say "a person", "a reader" or the protagonist's name. Capacity Gate and Demo Account are v2/infra-facing so absence is defensible; the Visitor drift is a small discipline slip in files that elsewhere police vocabulary hard. *Fix:* use `Visitor` where the spine means the glossary role.
- **low** Twelve FRs are cited nowhere: FR-16, FR-20, FR-21, FR-23, FR-24, FR-25, FR-26, FR-27, FR-30, FR-31, FR-33, FR-34. Most are v2 or Operator-facing and correctly out of a UX spine. Two are not: FR-16 is *satisfied* in substance by § The Token Contract but never claimed, and FR-34 is MVP scope (see §1). *Fix:* cite FR-16 at the head of § The Token Contract; handle FR-34 as above.

## 8. Shape fit — strong

DESIGN.md runs Brand & Style → Colors → Typography → Layout & Spacing → Elevation & Depth → Shapes → Components → *The Token Contract* → *Migrating the Anchor from SCSS to tokens* → Do's and Don'ts. All eight canonical sections are present and their relative order is exactly canonical. Note the review brief's premise is inverted on the facts: the two invented sections sit **between Components and Do's and Don'ts**, not after Do's-and-Don'ts. That does not break the order lock — the spec locks the order of canonical sections when present, and interleaving an invented section does not reorder them.

EXPERIENCE.md carries all eight required defaults (Foundation, Information Architecture, Voice and Tone, Component Patterns, State Patterns, Interaction Primitives, Accessibility Floor, Key Flows), plus both required-when-applicable sections, each properly triggered: Inspiration & Anti-patterns by the `nutlope/hallmark` reference named at intake, Responsive & Platform by mobile-first + the 360/390/760/1280 breakpoint set. Three invented sections, all earning their place: Seams Inventory and Asset Budget are named deliverables 6 and 2 of the UX prompt, and Open Items carries six live blockers with the epic each gates — the most directly consumable section in the pair for sprint planning.

### Findings

- **low** ~300 lines of token contract and migration sequence separate § Components from § Do's and Don'ts, so the hard-rules section — the part an implementer re-reads most — sits at the far end of the file behind two reference appendices. Order-legal, but it reads as an appendix rather than a summary. *Fix:* consider moving The Token Contract and Migrating the Anchor after Do's and Don'ts; nothing in the spec forbids it and the canonical eight then run uninterrupted.
