C2 · Display entrance, replaces GlitchText · Story 2.27

A heading that arrives, not a heading that malfunctions. Per-character reveal on opacity only, staggered by DOM index, capped at ~500ms. No text-shadow at any keyframe, no clip-path, no transform offset, no second hue, and no loop. The retired glitch-loop ran every six seconds forever; infinite loops are banned everywhere else in this system and this was the one instance. Reload to replay.

Cuatro Aberration, dropped, O-12 item 1

The accessible name is the heading's own text. No aria-label on a wrapper <div>, no aria-hidden on the element carrying the words. This fixes O-13.

The scrambling glyph pool is retired. Substituting random characters emits nonsense to anything watching the DOM and makes the accessible name unstable during the entrance.

C3 · Scrim, replaces ScanlineOverlay · Story 2.28 · closes O-12 item 2

The effect goes; the legibility job it was incidentally doing survives as a named role. The imagery below is deliberately bright and moving, because --c-scrim's guarantee is a worst case, not an average: composited over pure white it holds --c-ink at 13.51:1, and every other role clears its own floor too. It is the one translucent value in the system, and it is an exception precisely because its ground is unknown by design, the exact inverse of the alpha-hairline case the rule was written about. The alpha lives on --c-scrim, not on the role.

Every role clears its floor here. Worst case, over pure white: ink 13.51, focus 9.02, accent-hover 6.94, muted 5.41, accent 4.77. Accent is the binding role, and it is what sets the alpha at 0.88.

The guarantee belongs to the stack, not to the token. Anything painted at a z-level above the scrim is over the imagery, not over the scrim. A sticky header at --z-sticky above a scrim at --z-raised is the case that catches people, and it is the real constraint on HomeLayout.

One state. The shipped light/full prop retires: a scrim that varies is a scrim whose guarantee varies.

Never a surface. Not a card ground, not a section ground, not a vignette, not a hover state. Depth is lightness, then a hairline, then a rule.

C4 · Control, the rule most likely to be lost in translation

There is no filled control anywhere in this system, and every component library in the estate ships a filled button as its default and its most-documented example. Hover moves the border and nothing else. Tab to these: the ring appears in one frame, not over a duration.

C7 · Error surface, Story 2.30 · O-12 item 3, both branches

The numeral is currently a <p> reading 404 carrying aria-label="Error 404", and it is not aria-hidden. Because aria-label on a <p> sits on a generic role, what a screen reader announces today is not reliably Error 404. So the redundancy test is a real test with a real chance of going either way, and Story 2.30 runs it rather than assuming. Both branches are rendered below so the story cannot stall on whichever way it lands.
