- (change) CRITICAL FIX 1 - token namespace collision. The @theme inline adapter mapped Tailwind's --color-bg to var(--color-bg), the same name on both sides. - (change) CRITICAL FIX: the scrim contrast table was computed in the WRONG COLOUR SPACE and every number in it was wrong. CSS composites alpha in gamma-encoded sRGB (255(1-a) + c*a on the encoded bytes), not in linear light. Blending the two luminances instead gave a composite ground of 0.1215 where the correct value is 0.01718, wrong by a factor of seven. Recomputed and verified by hand: over pure white (the worst backdrop that can exist) ink is 13.51:1 not 5.29, focus 9.02:1 not 3.53, accent-hover 6.94:1 not 1.31, muted 5.41:1 not 2.12, accent 4.77:1 not 1.87. - (override) The rule 'no interactive element sits on a scrim' is WITHDRAWN.
