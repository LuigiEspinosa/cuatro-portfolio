### A. Spec-to-implementation reconciliation

**A1 · The published Tailwind adapter silently redefines `max-w-sm` through `max-w-2xl` in every
consumer.** *Severity: high. Disposition: fix now.*

`contracts/tailwind.css:76-85` maps the spacing scale onto *named* keys (`--spacing-sm`,
`--spacing-md`, `--spacing-2xl`, …). In Tailwind v4 a named `--spacing-<name>` takes precedence
over `--container-<name>` when resolving `max-w-<name>`. Reproduced independently on 2026-08-29
by compiling the adapter with the repository's own `tailwindcss` 4.3.3:

| utility | compiles to | value | stock Tailwind |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.max-w-sm` | `max-width: var(--s-sm)` | 0.75rem (12px) | 24rem (384px) |
| `.max-w-md` | `max-width: var(--s-md)` | 1rem (16px) | 28rem (448px) |
| `.max-w-2xl` | `max-width: var(--s-2xl)` | 4rem (64px) | 42rem (672px) |

`--container-md: 28rem` is emitted into the same stylesheet and loses. AD-14 has seven
repositories vendor this file, so every consumer that writes `max-w-md` gets a 16px container.
The failure is silent: nothing errors, the utility resolves, and the page reads as a styling
mistake in the consumer rather than a fault in the contract.

**This is the epic's defining cross-story defect.** Story 1-13 generated the adapter and Story
1-19 consumed it, and no instrument between them could see it: the Hub uses Sass and cannot
exercise a Tailwind utility at all, and `cs-tracker`'s own tests assert against the *text* of
`assets/css/app.css` rather than compiled output. The first instrument to see it was the
Operator looking at a screenshot of the deployed `cs-tracker.cuatro.dev`. Already filed as
**DW-15** (high, open) with **DW-19** recording that no gate can see it and that the shape of the
gate is a deferred decision.

**A2 · FR-17 is assigned to Epic 1 in the coverage map, is enforced by a story in Epic 2, and is
not met by the as-built.** *Disposition: fix now (spec reconciliation).*

`epics.md:753` reads `| FR-17 | 1 | Anchor consumes its own tokens (migration steps 1–2) |`, and
`epics.md:813` lists FR-17 among the FRs Epic 1 covers. But the story that actually enforces it is
`### Story 2.34: FR-17 conformance, no colour literal outside the contract`, in Epic 2 and

12. **Act on the `oversized` warning instead of carrying it.** It fired on 15 of 21 stories and
    no story was split. The signal is already computed by the loop and cost nothing to produce;
    what is missing is a rule that consumes it. *Source: F3.*

13. **Put the story key in every commit subject, including hand-driven work.** All eleven `feat:`
    commits in the epic's first half carry none, and per-story attribution for the whole
    ops/estate half had to be reconstructed from board comments. From Story 1-14 onward the
    bmad-loop template makes this automatic and the attribution is exact — the gap is the
    hand-driven half. *Source: F6.*

14. **When a story moves a host, treat "the credentials that describe it" as a set, not a
    field.** E1 is the general form: the plan named "an opaque host secret" singular, three
    secrets described the box, and the story updated the one it was told about. *Source: E1.*
