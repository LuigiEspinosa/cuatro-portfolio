edge-case and verification-gap lenses ran inline in this session over a **narrowed scope**, weighted to
`ops/traefik/` (compose, static and dynamic configuration, the suite), `ops/postgres/` (compose, the init

### A. Aggregate views

**Architecture delta.** Two stacks now stand outside the Anchor's `docker-compose.yml`, each started by
hand and never by a deploy: `ops/traefik/compose.yml` (project `traefik`, service `ingress` on 80, 443 and
loopback 8081, file provider only, no Docker socket) and `ops/postgres/compose.yml` (project `postgres`,
service `estate-postgres`, network `estate-postgres` that consumers join as `external`). Umami and the
tracker's three services join `estate-postgres` (`docker-compose.yml`, `networks:`); `cs-tracker` joins it
from its own repository (`ca75686`, `bde2b3f`). Routing still rides `cs-tracker_default`, owned by the
`cs-tracker` project, a recorded choice (`ops/routing-inventory.md` § The shared network). No cycle and no
layering breach: no application reaches another's database, and the init script revokes `CONNECT` from
`PUBLIC` on every consumer database and on `postgres` and `template1` (`ops/postgres/init/10-consumers.sh`).

**Duplication map.** Three restore-verify scripts now solve one problem three ways
(`ops/tracker-restore-verify.sh`, `ops/tournament-restore-verify.sh`, `ops/postgres-restore-verify.sh`), and
the one-flag defect Story 4-8 fixed in the first is still in the second (L7). Every runbook re-declares its
own `CF` helper and probe loop; the `HUB_TAG` export lived in two Epic 3 runbooks and was missing from the
Epic 4 one written in a separate session (M5). Noted; no consolidation proposed while the box halves are
done.

**Size growth.** Records, not code: `ops/caddy-retirement.md` 1,111 lines, `ops/traefik-cutover.md` 965,
`ops/postgres.md` 751, `deferred-work.md` +648. They carried this retrospective. The largest code file is
`ops/postgres-backup.sh` at 262 lines with one function per stage; not a god-class.

**Pattern divergence.** Umami's healthcheck is liveness only, where the tracker's gates on its database (L3).
Otherwise the new compose files follow the house rules: exact image pins (`traefik:v3.7.13`,
`postgres:18.6-trixie`, Umami by digest), no `container_name`, healthchecks, `.env` by name only.

**Spec-to-implementation reconciliation.** See M1 (the epic's deploy goal) and L1 (the tournament). The
rest reconciles through recorded decisions, each **accepted**: the in-place rebuild with Traefik beside Caddy
on 8443 and one Origin Rule per hostname (4-1, `ops/settled-inputs-refresh.md`); PostgreSQL 18 over 19;
Story 4.2's "certificate-age monitoring sees the new certificates" read as no monitor change under AD-26
(`ops/traefik-cutover.md` § How certificate monitoring sees this, confirmed as Pending action 3); www
answering 308 to non-GET methods (4-6); PITR deferred (4-5).
