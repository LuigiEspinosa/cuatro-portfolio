Round 01

The Awards Roulette & Prize Structure

Technique · Morphological Analysis — break the awards machine into mechanics, then recombine. The demo parser is the moat: it sees stats no human scoreboard ever could.

SPIN
THE
FATE
Press spin — luck sets the game

Two-stage draw — luck sets the game, skill wins it

The roulette randomly picks which categories are live; the demo stats then deterministically pick each winner. Categories stay mystery-blurred until the ceremony so nobody games toward a known award mid-tournament. The ceremony itself is a literal spinning-wheel reveal — a dramatic per-category flip, recap- and stream-ready.

// The Main Event — Synthesis

One Demo File to Rule It All

The session collapsed dozens of features into a handful of high-leverage moves. The breakthrough: most of the hard parts share a single source — the demo file — and most "fairness" features are really the same move wearing different jerseys.

The Keystone Insight

The demo file is the product keystone — one risk, one moat

The recorded match demo is simultaneously the stats source, the match referee for disputes and scores, and the provably-fair RNG seed. Solve demo ingestion once and three problems collapse into a single defensible moat.

Stats Source
Every award, leaderboard and Wrapped card flows from the parse.
Match Referee
Disputed scores resolve against the demo, not against arguments.
RNG Seed
seed = hash(final demo) → a draw anyone can reproduce.
🔑

Steam OAuth is a triple-win

One integration delivers login, SteamID64 stat-matching, and the reconciliation step — three problems, one button.

🎯

Manufacture spotlight for non-winners

Wrapped, nominations, the achievement log and the pity-roulette are one pattern: make sure everyone gets a moment, not just the fraggers.

Wildcard
🎲

The provably-fair draw

Among friends the real threat isn't bad stats — it's rigging accusations. A reproducible, published seed quietly removes the whole class of argument.

🤖

The Discord bot is the front door

The crowd already lives in Discord. Match DMs, polls and announcements make the bot the real entry point, not an afterthought.

// The Spine

Three Independent Reward Tracks

The core design principle: keep three parallel ladders running so a non-elite player always has something live to chase.

TRACK 01

Champion

Pure skill

The bracket. Win your matches, climb the double-elim tree, lift the trophy. The classic spine of any tournament.

TRACK 02

Awards Roulette

Luck + niche

Two-stage draw, four buckets, anti-sweep caps and rate awards. Even an early exit can spin into glory.

TRACK 03

Meta-games

For the crowd

Predictions, house-chips, fantasy fragger, streaks and map segments. You never had to frag to win.

⚡ Chosen Direction — the build order

v1 — Must-have
  • Roster + Steam OAuth (SteamID64)
  • Double-elim bracket — random seed, drag/tap advance, live updates, byes, no-show handling
  • Staged pending→approve demo ingestion as source of truth
  • Awards roulette — two-stage draw, 4 buckets, one-trophy-per-spin cap, rate + volume classes, anti-farm thresholds, provably-fair seed, spinning ceremony, pity roulette
  • Tournament-home timeline feed
  • Admin vs viewer roles
Later — The expansion
  • Predictions / house-chips
  • Discord bot front door
  • Player Wrapped + recap page
  • Season persistence — status tiers, map segments, achievement log, hall of fame
  • Fantasy fragger
  • Gentle / privacy mode
