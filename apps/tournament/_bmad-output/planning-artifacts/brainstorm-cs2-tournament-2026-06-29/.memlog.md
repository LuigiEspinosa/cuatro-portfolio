- (insight) Core design principle emerged: three independent reward tracks (skill Champion / luck+niche Awards roulette / meta-games) so non-elites always have a live ladder
- (insight) SYNTHESIS - The demo file is the product keystone: stats source + match referee for disputes/scores + provably-fair RNG seed (one risk = one moat). Steam OAuth is a triple-win: login + SteamID64 matching + reconciliation. Wrapped/nominations/achievement-log/pity-roulette are one move (manufacture spotlight for non-winners). Provably-fair seeded draw is the wildcard breakthrough (among friends the threat is rigging accusations, not bad stats). Discord bot is the real front door.
- (decision) CHOSEN DIRECTION - Build around three reward tracks (Champion / Awards Roulette / Meta-games). v1 must-have: roster + Steam OAuth (SteamID64), double-elim bracket (random seed, drag/tap advance, live updates, byes, no-show handling), staged pending-to-approve demo ingestion as source of truth, awards roulette (two-stage draw, 4 category buckets, one-trophy-per-spin cap, rate+volume award classes, anti-farm thresholds, provably-fair seed, spinning ceremony, pity roulette), tournament-home timeline feed, admin vs viewer. Later: predictions/house-chips, Discord bot front door, player Wrapped + recap page, season persistence (status tiers, map segments, achievement log, hall of fame), fantasy fragger, gentle/privacy mode.
