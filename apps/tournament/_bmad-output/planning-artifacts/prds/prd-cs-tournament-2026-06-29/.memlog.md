- (decision) ADR convention LOCKED = overkill-capped damage (PlayerHurt.HealthDamageTaken, max 100/victim/round) divided by rounds played; matches in-game scoreboard, PoC-verified 124.1. User-confirmed. Changing later breaks all ADR-based awards.
- (decision) Prize-budget cap DROPPED for v1 (addendum had flagged it open). Reason: prizes are token shirts from a friend's brand; app records winners only, no eligibility/cap logic. Stays consistent with locked winners-only/no-money decision.
- (decision) Gentle/privacy mode NOT NEEDED in v1 (addendum had flagged it required before shame awards). Reason: closed friends group, comedy/anti-skill awards curated by admin and played for laughs, no public exposure. Becomes a prerequisite only if the app is ever made public.
- (decision) Derived-stat rules pinned in PRD: entry frag = first kill after RoundFreezetimeEnd (opening death = first death); 1vX clutch = last-alive on team then wins round by elimination OR objective; KAST = Kill/Assist/Survived/Traded with 5s trade window; anti-farm floors = min 24 rounds played (HS%/rate also need min 20 kills); AFK/idle DQ excludes a match's stats when idle >=50% of rounds.
- (decision) Fairness rules pinned: match format + overtime + tie policy declared/stored/enforced pre-match (no ad-hoc changes), winner from demo score else admin manual entry logged; award-tie ladder = secondary stat > rate efficiency > head-to-head > earliest timestamp > shared co-winner trophy; walkover/forfeit contributes no stats (winner gets a badge not a phantom win), rate awards over rounds actually played in completed matches.
- (decision) Awards-roulette provably-fair design: seed = SHA-256 of the final (championship-deciding) match demo, published; deterministic PRNG drives only the luck stages (which categories are live + luck-meter bias); category winners are deterministic from the approved stat snapshot; one-trophy-per-spin cap resolves overflow via the deterministic award-tie ladder; whole ceremony reproducible from (demo + stat snapshot + algorithm).
- (assumption) AFK/idle DQ requires per-tick player position tracking (movement epsilon) which the Go PoC did not exercise; demoinfocs-golang exposes positions so it is feasible but unproven. Flagged as a parsing-extension risk for the Architecture phase.
