## What Went Well (keep doing this)

1. **"Keep the decision pure, leave only the live call in the closure."** Born in 5.1 (`kastQualified`), retrofitted under duress in 5.2's review (`addWeirdKills` after a transposed counter survived a green suite), then applied *up front* in 5.3 (`clutchTracker`) and 5.4 (`isIdleRound`). This is the epic's signature engineering pattern — the single choice that decided whether an AC was mutation-testable at all, given `demoInfoProvider` (`IsBlinded`/`IsAlive`/`GetTeam`/`Position`) panics on test-constructed players.
2. **Positive-proof GATES, not eyeballed numbers.** 5.3 formalized GATE 1 (prove the signal fires) / GATE 2 (prove an empty result *positively*); 5.4 escalated to GATE A/B for the catastrophic-blast-radius `Position()` signal — which fired **750,311 times** with a 1550-unit median displacement, disproving a tournament-wide false-DQ by measurement.
3. **Seams designed two stories ahead.** 5.6's `fetchFeedSnapshot` was built `id`-keyed with `aria-live` *specifically* so 5.8 could `router.refresh()` without wiping scroll. The read/consumer split paid off exactly as planned.
4. **The architectural payoff on 5.5.** Leaderboard as a plain `security_invoker` live view over `status='approved' AND idle_dq=false` closed Epic-4 Action Item #2 with **zero** change to `approve_match`/`rollback_match`. The status-flip *is* the recompute (AD-20) — the CQRS-lite bet from Epic 4 held.
5. **THE BAR keeps finding real bugs by execution.** Two *latent pre-epic* bugs corrected by measurement (3.3 over-count; dead `AttackerBlind`) — neither was arguable, both were measured.

## Key Insights / Breakthroughs

- **"A zero justified by tournament format is a red flag, not a proof."** The 1v1-wingman format was invoked to *explain* a zero twice and was wrong/nearly-wrong both times: 5.2a proved `blind 0` was a dead field (in a 1v1 the *enemy* flashes you — 172 detonations, 4 genuinely-blind kills all reading `AttackerBlind=false`); 5.4's review caught that a killed-before-moving duelist reads false-idle *most acutely in 1v1*, the opposite of the story's "5v5-only" framing. **Doctrine: measure zeros, never narrate them.** This is now cited by name across 5.3/5.4/5.6/5.7.
- **Author-side mutation tables keep missing survivors that reviewers find.** A transposed counter (5.2) passed both a green suite *and* THE BAR; 5.5 needed five mutation-survivor coverage patches; 5.7 needed three regression tests. The pre-review mutation rule works — but the *author-side* application is the weak link.
- **Verification narratives drifted ahead of evidence.** 5.3's AC5 `PARTIAL` (measurements real, three narrated claims unbacked), self-refuting arithmetic corrected at review, "independent reconciliation" that was actually a mirror of production logic. Several patches this epic fixed the *story writeup*, not the code.
- **Frontend live-QA lagged worker live-QA.** The worker got 14/14-demo measured gates; the frontend leaned on hand-seeded single-session checks until 5.8's StrictMode dead-subscription race — invisible to lint, build, and 461 node tests — forced two-session browser live-QA.

## What Was Hard (systems-level, no blame)

- **The trap-4 pre-match round** — the single most-repeated correctness hazard (5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4). MatchZy demos open with a played-then-discarded non-warmup round; a bare `counter++` double-counts it. Impact escalated: +1 kills (5.1), a **15× knife over-count** on a comedy award (5.2 — 14 of 15 raw knife kills sat at round index 0), 2-seen-1-committed duels (5.3). Each story re-taught the per-round `cur` scratch + assign-at-RoundEnd discipline in its Dev Notes.
- **The `demoInfoProvider` purity hazard** (5.2a, 5.3, 5.4) — live parser derefs panic/degrade on test-constructed players. The unbroken workaround (isolate the deref to the thinnest closure, pass plain values out) is what kept each classifier mutation-testable.
- **The `reparse.go` "second path" drop** — flagged in every worker story: a field mapped in `cli.go` but dropped in `reparse.go` writes correctly on first parse then zeroes on re-parse. Mitigated by widening both sites in one edit + mutation-testing both.
- **The 5.8 StrictMode dead-subscription bounce** — `removeChannel()` is async; StrictMode's sync setup→cleanup→setup returned the still-`leaving` channel and `subscribe()`'s `isClosed()` gate skipped wiring the listeners, leaving the subscription dead under `next dev`. Fixed by rewriting to a module-level per-tab singleton (ref-counted, `useSyncExternalStore`). Only provable by live-QA — the one story that bounced `review → in-progress → done`.

## Scope Corrections / Amendments

- **5.2a — the epic's defining amendment.** Exists solely because 5.2 *and its review* both accepted a false "proven by mode" zero for `blind_kills`. Origin of the project-wide "measure zeros" doctrine. Native parser state (`Killer.IsBlinded()`) permitted for this one column where the event field is *demonstrated* dead; `no_scope_kills` left at 0 deliberately (an ordinary 0-of-16 sample with no counter-evidence — do NOT "restore" a field without measured proof it's populated).
- **Don Clutch award retirement (5.3)** — an in-flight decision (retire the empty award, keep the bucket) that exceeded the story's written instruction ("flag it, don't solve it"), caught at review, retroactively authorized; the spec was amended to stop contradicting what shipped. The File List had omitted three modified UX/docs files — caught at review.
- **`Σk > Σd` conservation relax (5.4)** — the open Epic-3 action item, deliberately *pinned* to 5.4 (the only remaining Epic-5 story legitimately touching `validate.go`) and explicitly kept out of 5.2/5.3 scope.
- **5.4's HARDEN-NOW idle-survival guard** — a review-time scope expansion (`deaths[sid] > 0` ⇒ not-idle) rather than deferring a 1v1-acute correctness risk.

## Next Epic Preview — Epic 6: Awards Roulette — Producer & Verifier (CAP-6)

Eleven stories — the sharpest genre shift yet: a **Go producer (`worker/awards`) and a JS verifier (`lib/roulette`) that must agree byte-for-byte**. Adds the ceremony schema slice (`ceremony`, `spin`, `award`, `award_result`, `stat_snapshot`, `verification_bundle`).

- ✅ **The stat surface is complete** — core/weird/derived all in `stat_row`.
- ⚠️ **Verify at 6.2 planning:** AD-19 demands rate stats as **`{num, den}` integer pairs**, not floats. 5.5's leaderboard does sum-then-divide (`ADR 3000/38`), which *suggests* integer components are present in `stat_row` — but that contract was never explicitly pinned in Epic 5. A rounded float anywhere breaks the Go/JS byte-for-byte agreement. (Action Item #1.)
